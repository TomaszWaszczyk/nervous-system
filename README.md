# nervous-system

Studies show oxytocin, the “tend and befriend” hormone, not only calms stress but promotes long-term cardiovascular and immune health.

And when we connect, our bodies shift into repair and restore mode. It’s not just emotional—it’s biological. Connection is your second nervous system.
